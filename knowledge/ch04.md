## 1. Concepts (what the chapter teaches, in our words)

- An edge is a rapid brightness change; two detectors: a large **first derivative** (gradient) or a **zero-crossing of the
  second derivative** (Laplacian). Floe boundaries feed area/perimeter/shape and the floe size distribution (Ch5–Ch8).
- Gradient `∇f = [Gx, Gy]ᵀ` (4.1), magnitude `sqrt(Gx² + Gy²)` (4.2), direction `arctan(Gx/Gy)` as printed (4.3), and
  the cheaper `Gx² + Gy²` (4.4) / `|Gx| + |Gy|` (4.5) forms. Book `x` = row, `y` = column (Ch2 convention).
- Running differences (4.6a/b, Fig. 4.1) sit at half-pixel positions → symmetric 3×3 kernels: **Sobel** (4.7a/b,
  Fig. 4.2(b), centre weight 2 = built-in smoothing, preferred) and **Prewitt** (4.8a/b, Fig. 4.2(c)); all sum to zero.
  Edge = `|∇f| > T`; the book's `T = 0.05` (Fig. 4.3) is only meaningful with MATLAB `edge`'s conventions (`/8`
  kernels, replicate padding, thinning) on `double(im)/256`.
- Laplacian `∇²f` (4.9) from second differences (4.10–4.12) → 4-neighbour kernel `[0 1 0; 1 −4 1; 0 1 0]` (4.13,
  Fig. 4.4(a)) and the 8-neighbour `[1 1 1; 1 −8 1; 1 1 1]` (Fig. 4.4(b)); zero-crossings via a 3×3 max/min-difference
  rule (p. 64); noise-sensitive → smooth first.
- Gaussian `Gσ` (4.14; truncate at 3σ ≈ 99 %) and **LoG** `∇²Gσ = (x²+y²−2σ²)/(2πσ⁶)·exp(…)` (4.15). Fig. 4.5's printed
  kernels are `fspecial('gaussian',5,1)` / `fspecial('log',5,1)` (unit-sum Gaussian, mean-subtracted LoG) — not raw
  samplings of Eq. 4.15. Fig. 4.6 = `edge(crop,'log',0.005,2)` (13×13 kernel = `2·ceil(3σ)+1`).
- Morphology (§4.2): a flat symmetric **structuring element** with its origin at the centre; Fig. 4.7(c) "disk with
  5-pixel radius" is the printed 9×9/69-px matrix = MATLAB `strel('disk',5)` (n = 4 periodic-line approximation),
  (d) = `strel('diamond',5)`.
- Binary erosion `A ⊖ B = {z | (B)z ⊆ A}` (4.16–4.17) and dilation with the **reflected** SE `A ⊕ B = {z | (B̂)z ∩ A ≠ ∅}`
  (4.18–4.19); grayscale erosion = min over the SE (4.20), dilation = max over the reflected SE (4.21). Fig. 4.8 (DIPUM):
  an 11×15 rectangle eroded by a cross → one row of 5; dilated → 5×9 with clipped corners (unit-test fixtures).
- Closing `(A ⊕ B) ⊖ B` (4.22, fills dark details smaller than B, "push down from above", Fig. 4.11) and opening
  `(A ⊖ B) ⊕ B` (4.23, removes bright details, "push up from below", Fig. 4.12).
- Reconstruction: geodesic dilation `D_G^(1)(F) = (F ⊕ B) ∩ G` (4.25), iterate `X_k = (X_{k−1} ⊕ B) ∩ G` until stable
  (4.27) = `R_G^D(F)`; dual by erosion `(F ⊖ B) ∪ G` (4.28–4.30); grayscale with pointwise min/max (4.32–4.34,
  4.36–4.38; Figs. 4.13–4.14). Requires marker ⊆ mask (4.24/4.31). B = the connectivity (MATLAB default 8).
- Morphological gradients: basic `(A ⊕ B) − (A ⊖ B)` (4.39), internal `A − (A ⊖ B)` (4.40), external `(A ⊕ B) − A`
  (4.41), `ρ_int + ρ_ext = ρ` (4.42). Half-gradients are thinner (width = r); the basic ring is 2r wide.
- §4.3: Sobel ≈ Prewitt; LoG noisy; grayscale gradient "derivative-like" → only Sobel and the **binary internal
  gradient** are compared. Isolated floes: both fine. Touching floes: Sobel leaves weak non-closed edges (lower T = 0.03
  adds noise, Fig. 4.19); the internal gradient closes edges but loses weak ones (r = 5) or breaks weak connections while
  shrinking floes (r = 16, Fig. 4.20). Neither separates tightly connected floes → Ch5 watershed / Ch6 GVF.
- Text-vs-code parameter mismatches: figures use r = 15 (Figs. 4.9–4.16; Fig. 4.15 caption "157" is a typo — 157 is the
  pixel count of `strel('dis',7)`), r = 5/16 (Figs. 4.17–4.20); `morphology.m` uses r = 7 on the full image.

## 4. MATLAB→Python lessons learned (new pitfalls; each is a row in `function_map.md`)

1. **MATLAB `edge` sobel/prewitt is much more than Eq. 4.2 > T**: kernels `fspecial('sobel')/8` (`/6` Prewitt) on a
   `'replicate'`-padded image, `b = bx² + by²` compared with `T²` (auto: `4·mean(b)`, returned `thresh = sqrt`), then
   **thinning** by the compiled `computeEdges`: with `bx = |bx|`, `by = |by|` and `b` **zero-padded** by one pixel,
   `e = b > cutoff & ((bx >= kx·by − 100eps & b(r,c−1) <= b(r,c) & b(r,c) > b(r,c+1)) | (by >= ky·bx − 100eps &
   b(r−1,c) <= b(r,c) & b(r,c) > b(r+1,c)))`. The multipliers sit on the *other* component (`'horizontal'` → kx = 0),
   ties keep the first pixel of a ridge, and Prewitt **divides after filtering** (`imfilter(a, op)/6`, 1 ulp visible on
   93 px of `test.jpg` if `/6` is folded into the kernel). Replicate padding of `b` (the analysis's first guess) differs
   at the right/bottom border — only visible with `'vertical'`. Roberts: masks `[1 0; 0 −1]/2`, scale 6, offsets
   `[−1 1 1 −1]`. `skimage.filters.sobel` (magnitude only) and `skimage.feature.canny` are unrelated.
2. **`edge('log')` thresholds the jump across the zero crossing** (`|b(p) − b(q)| > T`, `2T` for exact zeros), on the
   negative side, interior only; `op −= mean(op)`; auto `T = 0.75·mean|b|`. On piecewise-constant synthetic images the
   result depends on the sign of ~1e-17 rounding noise (MATLAB's `imfilter` summation order) → `near`; exact on real
   images. The p. 64 3×3 max/min rule is a *different* detector (`laplacian_zero_crossings`).
3. **`double(im)/256` is not `im2double`** (`/255`): the book's `T = 0.05` and the automatic threshold change with `/255`.
   Port the literal scaling; `edge` refuses integer/bool input so the caller must choose.
4. **`fspecial('log')` ≠ Eq. 4.15 sampled**: unit-sum Gaussian × `(x²+y²−2σ²)/σ⁴`, then mean-subtracted (sum 0, centre
   −0.3182 for 5×5 σ = 1 vs the analytic −1/π). `'gaussian'` zeroes entries `< eps·max` before normalising; omitted
   `hsize` with given `sigma` → `2·ceil(2σ)+1`; `edge('log')` uses `2·ceil(3σ)+1`. `'laplacian'(α)` divides by `α+1`.
5. **`strel('disk', r)` is an octagon** (n = 4 periodic-line decomposition, Adams 1993): r = 5 → 9×9/69 px (the printed
   Fig. 4.7(c)), r = 7 → 13×13/157, r = 15 → 29×29/697, r = 16 → 31×31/817; a Euclidean disk (`skimage.morphology.disk`,
   `cv2.MORPH_ELLIPSE`, or `n = 0`) is a different shape (r = 5: 11×11/81). `strel` accepts unambiguous **prefixes**
   (`'dis'`; `'di'` errors). `imerode`/`imdilate` with the decomposed sequence equal the flat-nhood result (Minkowski-sum
   identity) — use the sequence for speed on 12-Mpx images. Origin = `floor((size+1)/2)`, also for even SEs.
6. **Border rule and reflection**: erosion pads with 1 / intmax / +Inf, dilation with 0 / intmin / −Inf **and uses the
   reflected SE** (Eqs. 4.18/4.21) — invisible for symmetric disks, decisive for asymmetric/even SEs (`cv2.erode/dilate`
   are non-reflected forms; the port flips the kernel and maps the anchor to `(M−1−r0, N−1−c0)` for dilation).
7. **`imclose` is not the bare composition**: `imclose.m` pre-pads by `ceil(size(getnhood(se))/2)` with **0**
   (`padarray(A, padSize, 'both')`), dilates, erodes, crops — so dark notches open to the border are not closed.
   `morphop_fast.m` routes SEs with nnz < 600, every side ≤ 15 and not an all-ones rectangle to a Halide kernel whose
   pad is the **class minimum** (−Inf / intmin / false). Both coincide for uint8/logical/non-negative double; they differ
   (80 px on a signed field) only for negative values. `imopen.m` applies `imdilate(imerode(A))` unpadded. Gated by
   `images.UseHalide` (= 1 by default). 43 of 54 closings failed before this was reproduced.
8. **scipy `minimum_filter`/`maximum_filter` route `cval` and the accumulation through float64**: for int64/uint64 the
   intmax pad overflowed to intmin (erosion pad became −∞) and values > 2⁵³ are corrupted → use numpy pad + shift-and-
   reduce in the image's own dtype. Matters for ch5: `skimage.measure.label` returns **int64** label images.
9. **MATLAB R2025a rejects int64/uint64 in `imerode`/`imdilate`** ("Expected input … to be one of: single, double,
   int8, int16, int32, uint8, uint16, uint32, logical") → those dtypes can only be verified at L1 (literal loops,
   cross-backend equality); label images should be cast to int32 before any MATLAB reference run.
10. **`logical − logical` is `double`** in MATLAB (`BW1 = I − J` → 0/1 double), `uint8 − uint8` saturates at 0 (exact
    here because `J ⊆ I ⊆ K`); a generic `A − B` on uint8 must clip (`_matlab_minus`).
11. **`imreconstruct` needs `marker <= mask`** (MATLAB errors; the port raises) and a 3×3 `conn` (the port also takes any
    nhood). skimage `reconstruction` computes the same fixed point (different algorithm, identical result) — `exact`.
    Reconstruction by erosion has **no MATLAB builtin** → `reimplemented`, checked via the complement identity.
12. **`bwareaopen(BW, P)` keeps components with `>= P` pixels** (8-conn); `skimage.morphology.remove_small_objects`
    changed its threshold parameter in 0.26 → built on `label_components` instead.
13. **`medfilt2` zero-pads** (`mode='constant', cval=0`); `medfilt2(..., 'symmetric')` = scipy `reflect`. `conv2`'s
    default is `'full'` (a 4290×2856 map becomes 4294×2860) — keep MATLAB's size when porting commented-out lines.
14. **`edge` on the compiled path accepts uint8 with different scaling**, and `'log'` converts with `im2single` — the port
    takes float only; a `single` MATLAB image is matched only to single precision (float32 → float64 promotion).
15. **Crop-then-process vs process-then-crop**: "Figure X of Figure 4.3(a)" means crop first; the r = 15 disk / replicate
    padding differ within 15 px / 1 px of the crop border. Locate crops by NCC against the (inverted) PDF bitmap.
16. **Analysis pre-checks are not references, again**: the replicate-padded thinning matched `test.jpg` with 0 px by
    luck (no right-column candidates); the constructed-tie fixtures in `compat.mat` exposed the zero padding.
17. **nbconvert on this host**: the `python3` kernelspec resolves to Anaconda's python unless `.venv/Scripts` is prepended
    to `PATH` before `python -m jupyter nbconvert --execute` — otherwise the notebook runs against the wrong site-packages
    (missing `seaice` deps or a different numpy) and fails or silently uses different versions.
